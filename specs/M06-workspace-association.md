# Explicit workspace association

An association is a deliberate sharing grant to all current and future members of the chosen workspace. Require current membership and authenticated client/provider identity from canonical creation under the persisted deployment policy. Never infer authority from a supplied address or copied draft. Require explicit audience confirmation tied to the exact policy/creation fingerprint; no automatic linking.

Store immutable creation provenance independently of canonical projection rebuilds. Duplicate identical grants are no-ops; different proof cannot replace an existing grant. Orphaned or replacement creation makes reads unavailable. Current membership and session chain authorize scoped reads only after association. Never expose private consent signatures or enable transactions. Indexed source/freshness must remain explicit; synthetic does not mean live verified or spendable.

The current immutable implementation has no unshare control; disclose that before confirmation. Browser acceptance remains pending. Verification and remaining work: implementation-records/M06-association-2026-10-23.md.
